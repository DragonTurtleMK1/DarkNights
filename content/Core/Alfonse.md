# Early Life

Little is known about the childhood of Alfonse, however, during his young adulthood, he joined up with the German auxiliary, quickly rising the ranks and becoming a [Hessian Grenadeer.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hessian_(soldier))  

## Military Career and Death

He survived several battles in the Southern American theatre before eventually succumbing to a colonial soldier in the [Siege of Yorktown.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Siege_of_Yorktown) However, that was not the end for Alfonse, in the chaos, that soldier would have drained him of blood, and in the process of intermingling essences, given Alfonse a new life as a [[Vampires|Vampire]].  

## Burgeoning Un-life

The early days of any [[Vampires|Vampire]] are difficult, but Alfonse took well to the trials and tribulations that he found pressed upon his new existence.
