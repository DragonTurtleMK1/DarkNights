Joe Greene, born "Joseph Green", was a player character from Dark Knights in San Francisco. He was a bitten-born werewolf.
## Childhood and early life
Joe was born in New York City in 1840 into a big Irish-Catholic family. He was always large for his age, as well as quiet and stoic. From a young age, he picked up the mentality that if someone punches you, you punch them back.

His family did not anticipate that he had the potential for higher education. Joe dropped out of school at sixth grade, and was expected the working world like most of the men in his family by the age of 13.

Joe usually found odd jobs doing manual labor like loading and carrying heavy crates and barrels. His imposing size also gained him security jobs as he entered adulthood.

## Joining the Irish mafia
With a reputation for being good in a fight and minding his own business, Joe's services were recommended to the growing Irish mafia. He joined the payroll as security and bodyguard depending on the job. It paid enough for him to look the other way when anything morally questionable occurred.

During this time, he became acquainted with the crooked detective John O'Donovan.

## Shootout and lycanthropy incident
One day in the year of 1869, Joe was part of a security escort through the New York harbor. His group was ambushed by another gang, and a massacre ensued. Once the dust settled, Joe discovered that he was the sole survivor of the carnage. He had also been bitten by a huge wolf-like creature during the fight.

## Flight from New York
#### Fond farewell
Word spread about the fight that broke out at the docks. Detective O'Donovan found out that Joe was expected to take the fall both with the gangs and the cops.

O'Donovan managed to find Joe first and get him onto a train heading out west. It would be the last time the two would see each other alive.
#### First full moon
Joe left New York with only the clothing on his back and what cash he had on-hand. He hopped the rails towards California, which would be a tense month-long journey.
A full moon occurred while he was between trains.
Joe painfully transformed into a huge, hulking, shaggy monster similar to the things that attacked his convoy back in New York.
He felt his anger and fear well up inside of him, but he did not lose control of his faculties.

## Arrival in San Francisco
Joe arrived in California in shredded clothing and clearly haunted by his past. He also had little money left. With nowhere to go and not feeling comfortable being around people, he had to find an unconventional place to lay low.
### Joining the railyard
On the outskirts of the San Francisco rails, Joe found unused boxcars that he took to sleeping in. Other vagrants and strays started trickling in, and Joe could not bear to send them away. He took to being their quiet protector as long as they left him alone and didn't look into his full moon activities.

With his build and capabilities, underground fighting rings became a consistent stream of income. It helped that he could bounce back from almost any injury.
### Meeting Miss Harp
While Joe was not easily harmed, his clothing regularly would be destroyed. He learned of a woman who could mend wounds in the middle of the night as well as repair clothing without any questions.
Joe found his way to Miss Harp's doorway and quickly became a regular patron, sheepishly presenting his ruined garments on a somewhat frequent basis.

Joe came into contact with three others in the supernatural community who eventually became something of a cohort. While reluctant at first, he eventually came to become ride or die with these individuals.

During this time, he also grew close to Miss Harp and Κυκλάδες.

## Relationships

### Player characters

#### Alfonse
Alfonse was a foil to Joe in almost every way, as Alfonse is a refined vampire with social status. The two did have the similar trait of not regretting killing.

Alfonse saved Joe's life in a pivotal moment, and Joe considered himself owing a life debt.

Alfonse also sponsored Joe's daughters going to cotillions.
#### Mira
Joe found Mira reliable, though wearing her status of a neophyte in her sleeve.

### Romantic interests

#### John O'Donovan
Joe and Detective O'Donovan regularly crossed paths as Joe entered the underworld scene. O'Donovan was friendly in a way that came across more sincere than Joe's other underworld contacts. Joe let his guard down around the other man, something he rarely did.

As time went on, Joe felt something like attraction to O'Donovan. He did not know how to address his feelings. 

When Joe had to leave O'Donovan and New York behind, it hurt him to his core. He had to shelve his feelings as he navigated his new existence in San Francisco.

Joe later received O'Donovan's badge from Saint Nicholas. This event shook Joe to his core.

During a moment of duress, the badge activated, summoning O'Donovan's ghost. Joe hurt to see his old flame in such a state. It also rekindled Joe's suppressed interest in the other man.

Joe agonized over O'Donovan's state, his own lingering feelings, and how the situation may affect his budding relationships with Selia and Κυκλάδες. His partners assured him that he could do whatever he needed to achieve peace of mind. Κυκλάδες also informed him that there was not much they could do to successfully free O'Donovan's ghost. At the very least, his bound existence shouldn't be torturous.

The next time Joe summoned O'Donovan, they had a sensual experience together. They also agreed that the past was the past and they would not pursue a long-term romance.

Joe named his firstborn son John in O'Donovan's memory.

When John was a child, he discovered the badge in a drawer. He accidentally summoned O'Donovan, something that no one thought could happen. In light of this development (and with John's interest in the supernatural), O'Donovan became John's godfather.

O'Donovan's badge passed between family members for a time after John reached adulthood, and by the 1950's, it was secured from John's descendants by Selia and Κυκλάδες for a trip they were taking with Joe to New York City. They gave him the item as a surprise, intending for him to have a reunion with his old flame. Selia went on a "girl's night" with the local selkies, and Κυκλάδες went hunting vampires (and any werewolves that might give Joe trouble for returning to the city).

When O'Donovan manifested, he took a physical form, which was an unexpected departure from the usual ethereal form he took. Because of this turn of events, Joe and O'Donovan were given the opportunity to go on the date they never were able to have back in the 1860's. They went to an ice cream parlor, and were able to kiss in an alleyway.

This is the last time that Joe and O'Donovan would see each other, as O'Donovan's badge would soon go missing after being returned to John's descendants.
#### [[Miss Harp|Selia Greene neé Harp]]
Joe met Selia Harp (known as "[[Miss Harp]]") in 1870, and quickly became quietly smitten with her. He would often come to her to fix his clothing after his outings as a werewolf, and usually would do so in a sheepish manner. Selia was always patient and understanding with Joe, never asking him any pressing questions about what he got up to that left his clothes in such a state. He grew fond of her, though understood that she had her own quiet troubles.

At one point, Selia asked Joe and the party to retrieve a seal pelt for her. After they completed this task, Joe learned that Selia was a selkie. Her personality also brightened a bit after she received her pelt.

He eventually took Selia on a date to a large social gathering. He was nearly killed multiple times by hostile forces at this gathering, but the experiences gave him the perspective to pursue his romantic interests. Once the events of the campaign calmed down, he began officially courting Selia.

The two got along well, and married a few months into Selia's first pregnancy.

Selia was initially an unmarried woman who had her own practice and house during a time in the US where that was uncommon in most of the country. She continued to have feminist pursuits after marrying Joe. He took little issue with it. As a man who grew up in poverty, women working outside of the house normal for him. He also did complain when Selia taught their sons skills that were considered gendered at the time (like cooking and sewing).

Selia would age in line with Joe, and so despite her original fae vigor, she would gray and wrinkle when Joe would. She also would  only last about ten more years after Joe's death. When she was ready to go, she collapsed into seafoam.
#### Κυκλάδες (aka Cid Greene)
Κυκλάδες (or "Cyclades") came into Joe's life like an unexpected burst of springtime. The satyr always seemed enthusiastic to see Joe when they would cross paths, and he would directly address Joe more often than he would anyone else in the party.

Joe did not know what to think of Κυκλάδες's cherry attitude and at first believed that the satyr was trying to mess with him. Joe's attitude softened as time went on, the shift solidifying after Κυκλάδες asked for help finding a job. Joe not only recommended becoming a mailman, but helped Κυκλάδες learn how to read English. They grew closer over the course of the writing lessons.
