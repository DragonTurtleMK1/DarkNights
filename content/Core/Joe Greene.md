Joe Greene, born "Joseph Green", was a player character from Dark Knights in San Francisco.
## Childhood and early life
Joe was born in New York City in 1840 into a big Irish-Catholic family. He was always large for his age, as well as quiet and stoic. From a young age, he picked up the mentality that if someone punches you, you punch them back.

His family did not anticipate that he had the potential for higher education. Joe dropped out of school at sixth grade, and was expected the working world like most of the men in his family by the age of 13.

Joe usually found odd jobs doing manual labor like loading and carrying heavy crates and barrels. His imposing size also gained him some security jobs.
## Joining the Irish mafia
With a reputation for being good in a fight and not one to ask questions, Joe's services were recommended to the growing Irish mafia.
