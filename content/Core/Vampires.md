# What is a Vampire?

Vampires are a broad category defined by their need to feed from mortal essence, the part of the soul that works magic and keeps their body animate.  This manifests in different ways, from feeding on emotions, to the standard drinking blood and living in castles.

These vampires are broken into different groups called Castes, unlike the castes of our world, they are not set rigidly above or below one another. 

The last thing that separates vampires from mortals, is that once, they were just like mortals, having a very human outlook on things, at least in the beginning of their new lives.

## What do Vampires Believe?

Vampires segregate themselves into groups, most believing that they are "born" into their new lives with a culture and spirit that guides them into the arms of their brethren.
