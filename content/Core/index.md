This world is much like our world that we live in, however in the shadows lurks [[Faeries]], [[Vampires]], [[Undead]], [[Goblins]] and [[Werewolves]]. 

These forces are hidden, some by choice, some in fear of reprisal by humans.  However, paramount among the reasons these creatures stay hidden, is human resistance to accepting that there is more to their world than they believe.  Human ignorance, and willingness to accept the world is as they believe it is, lies at the core of the setting.
