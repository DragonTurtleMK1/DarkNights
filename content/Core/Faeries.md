# Fae in the World

Faeries find themselves in an interesting place, being created by the convergence of belief and natural forces, they are all bound by rules.  Fae are varied, but most fall under two important rules.  [[Faeries#Rule Of The Word|The Rule of Word]], and [[Faeries#Rule Of The Material|The Rule of Material]], these two concepts bind and shape Faerie society. 

## Society

The world of the Fae is split into two main governing bodies, the [[Seelie Court]] and the [[Unseelie Court]] rule like governments, Faeries find themselves to be citizens of the courts, moving between the two is incredibly difficult but possible.  This is due to the rigid hierarchy, established by a complex network of socialization and favors owed.
